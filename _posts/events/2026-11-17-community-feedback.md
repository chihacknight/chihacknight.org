This event will take a different shape in the form of a community feedback session.

Join us to meet in different groups and talk about facilitated discussion prompts around Chi Hack Night, civic tech and our greater community.

Community feedback will be summarized and shared out in a future blog post. Join us Tuesday at 6:00 p.m. in person!
