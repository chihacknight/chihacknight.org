In June 2025, Derek Eder was [appointed to be a Trustee for the Village of Oak Park](https://derekeder.com/blog/oak-park-village-board), a suburb west of Chicago with 54,000 residents known for being ethnically diverse and socially liberal.

Derek will talk about what is involved in being a local legislator, the lessons he’s learned in his first 10 months, and share how his experience with Chi Hack Night and civic technology has prepared him and translated into policymaking.
