In 2019, at his first Chi Hack Night, Cook County Assessor Fritz Kaegi committed to building a fairer, and transparent property assessment system.

Seven years later, join Fritz for a look at how data and technology have transformed property assessments in Cook County. Hear how reforms led by the Assessor’s data science team have delivered $1.9 billion in property tax savings for owners of lower and middle-priced homes.

Learn how a single transparency tool, known as PTAXSIM created three legislative wins. Get insight on the expanded public data catalog including what assessment data is now available to the public and how it can be used.

Whether you’re a data scientist, civic technologist, academic researcher, or simply interested in using technology for public good, learn how assessment data can inform policy decisions. and inspire new tools to help make Cook County a more vibrant place to live, work, and thrive.
