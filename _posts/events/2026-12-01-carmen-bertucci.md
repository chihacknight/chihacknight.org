The common refrain is AI needs more datacenters, and for the large frontier language models this is true. But AI can deliver plenty of benefit without using those LLM's. This presentation will focus on what can be done with a smaller, more responsible footprint. Benefits as well as honest limitations of this approach will be reviewed here. It's unfair to expect Claude Opus quality without a million dollars' worth of servers, but a focused, calibrated setup can produce significant output running on modest hardware.

Recent improvements made to smaller, open-source models allow users to run capable AI on varying levels of hardware. Different types of solutions will be discussed with realistic expectations of the capabilities available at each level of hardware. Specific examples will be reviewed, and all information will be actionable: all models are found on https://www.huggingface.co, and software required to run them on https://github.com.
